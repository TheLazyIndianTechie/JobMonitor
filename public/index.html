IGG Job Monitor

Finds videogame-industry job vacancies in India and shares the good ones with the right WhatsApp Community groups.

The project covers studios and publishers, external QA and testing providers, art and animation outsourcing, localisation providers, gaming technology vendors and other industry service providers. Every job is checked, de-duplicated and approved by a person before it can be shared.

How it works

Each vacancy moves through six steps, in order.

  1. DiscoverPull listings from job sources and record where each one came from.
  2. FilterKeep jobs from approved employers, in relevant roles, that are open to people in India.
  3. VerifyConfirm the vacancy is genuine and still open. A page that loads is not enough; the check needs evidence about that specific vacancy.
  4. De-duplicateMatch on employer plus requisition ID or application URL, and flag likely duplicates by title and location.
  5. RouteSend each job to one Community group by its discipline. Jobs with a missing or conflicting mapping are held.
  6. ApproveA person approves, rejects or holds every job before it can be distributed.

Approval workflow

A job can be PENDING, APPROVED, REJECTED or HOLD. Approval and publishing are separate: approving a job never sends a message.

  • A first decision is applied straight away and written to the audit log.
  • An unchanged decision does nothing.
  • A changed decision is blocked until Reprocess_Confirmed is set to TRUE.
  • A confirmed change records the old and new decision, then resets the confirmation flag.

Roadmap

The next work is the distribution side. Each stage starts only after the one before it passes.

  1. WhatsApp feasibility. Test a gateway locally and read-only, then check Community subgroups can be addressed.
  2. Controlled message test. One labelled message to a designated test group, after explicit authorisation.
  3. Distribution eligibility. Allow only active, verified and approved jobs with exactly one enabled group mapping.
  4. Message formatter. Consistent posts with title, employer, location, work arrangement, experience and application link.
  5. Posting and tracking. Log every attempt and outcome, and prevent repeat sends.
  6. Monitoring and reporting. Show posted jobs, failed sends, pending approvals and routing conflicts.
  7. Scheduling and end-to-end validation. Production activation needs separate approval.
