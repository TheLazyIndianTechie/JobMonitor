IGG Job Monitor

Finds videogame-industry job vacancies in India and shares the good ones with the right WhatsApp Community groups.

What it does

  1. DiscoversListings from studios, publishers and industry service providers.
  2. FiltersBy employer, role and India eligibility.
  3. VerifiesThat the vacancy is genuine and still open.
  4. Removes duplicatesAnd keeps a record of where each listing came from.
  5. RoutesEach job to the matching Community group by discipline.
  6. Waits for approvalA human approves every job before anything is shared.

How it is built

Google SheetsThe database: jobs, sources, employers, group mappings, the approval queue and logs.
Google Apps ScriptThe automation engine that runs each step.
WhatsApp postingPlanned, not built. A self-hosted gateway is being tested.

Status

  • The approval workflow has passed controlled end-to-end tests.
  • Discovery, verification, duplicate checks and the database writer are developed and tested, but no genuine vacancies are being collected or inserted.
  • Nothing is posted to WhatsApp yet.
